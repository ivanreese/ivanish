I'm going through some changes in my life that I'd like to write my way through, mostly for myself, but also to share with _trusted friends_. Therefore I've decided to keep a little semi-private, occasionally-updated journal.

In the past I would've made unlinked pages on my website — hiding in plain sight. But my website's git repo is public, and LLMs scrape and fold into their sordid dossiers.

What's a lil coffeescript girlie to do!? 💅🏻

I can use encryption! There'll be a password! I'll give that password to _trusted friends_.

Reasoning:

* It's fun! I can be weird about it.
* I can control who is reading, and let more people in over time.
* If someone cracks the encryption, hopefully I'll learn how they did it, which is cool.
* And if someone cracks the encryption and, say, shares my secrets, that's _fine_. People are going to find out eventually.

Are you a trusted friend? Would you like to be? Message me somewhere private and I may give you the password.

I'll see you soon.
